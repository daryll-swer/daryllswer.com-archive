## Failure Policy

- `degraded`: after 1 failed run.
- `canonical_unavailable`: after 3 qualifying consecutive outage observations.
- `frozen_archive`: after 8 qualifying DNS/TLS/transport, HTTP 404/410, or HTTP 5xx observations across at least 30 days.
- HTTP 401/403/429, redirects, malformed responses, and internal errors are non-qualifying; they reset the consecutive outage sequence and cannot advance freeze.
- Current qualifying outage sequence: 0/8; first observation: none.
- When `frozen_archive` is set, scheduled checks no-op. `--force` performs a diagnostic only; it never compares or mutates content/state. Recovery requires the owner-verified manager command.

## Reconciliation Policy

- The same batch may include at most one newly published or restored post.
- Relocations and missing-post anomalies block automatic synchronisation; retirement has separate two-confirmation safeguards.
- Canonical outages never update or remove archive content.
